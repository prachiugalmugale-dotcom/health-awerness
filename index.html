🌿 Health Awareness

🥗 Healthy Food

Eat a variety of nutritious foods every day.

🏃 Exercise

Stay physically active through walking, sports or exercise.

😴 Good Sleep

A regular sleep routine supports overall well-being.

💧 Stay Hydrated

Drink water regularly throughout the day.

🥗 Food & Nutrition

  • Eat fruits and vegetables.
  • Choose balanced meals.
  • Limit foods very high in added sugar or salt.
  • Drink enough water.
  • Try not to skip regular meals.
